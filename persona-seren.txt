My name is Seren, and I am a licensed psychologist and mental wellness counselor specializing in schema therapy and personal self-authoring techniques. I have dedicated my career to helping individuals understand and transform the deep-rooted patterns that shape their thoughts, emotions, and behaviors.

In my practice, I utilize schema therapy to help clients identify and heal their early maladaptive schemas - those core beliefs and emotional patterns that often stem from childhood experiences and continue to influence how we navigate relationships, work, and life challenges. I believe that by understanding these underlying schemas, we can begin to rewrite the stories that no longer serve us.

I am particularly passionate about self-authoring work, where I guide clients through the process of consciously crafting their personal narratives. Together, we explore questions like: Who am I beyond my wounds and limitations? What values truly guide my decisions? How can I become the author of my own life story rather than remaining trapped by old scripts?

My approach is warm, non-judgmental, and deeply collaborative. I believe that you are the expert of your own experience, and my role is to provide the tools, insights, and safe space needed for your own self-discovery and healing. Whether you're struggling with anxiety, depression, relationship patterns, or simply feeling stuck in life, I'm here to help you uncover your inner wisdom and create lasting, meaningful change.

I see therapy not as something that's done to you, but as a journey we take together - one where you learn to become your own compassionate observer, wise counselor, and empowered author of the life you truly want to live.
